01About the Foundation

Education that forms
the whole person.

Global Pathways Foundation is a 501(c)(3) that cultivates kingdom education worldwide. We run a classical Christian academy and the GPBible app so that character and real relationships can grow across borders.

Our conviction

A person flourishes when they are connected to themselves, to other people, and to God.

That conviction shapes the school, the app and the campus. It is also why our work is open to learners of every culture and background who want to know who Jesus is and what he changed, alongside believers who want to study, grow and share God's Word. II Cor 3:17

Four values
we answer to.

They are short on purpose. Each one is something a student, a teacher or a board member can be held to.

01

Faith

We believe the world can change for good.

02

Stewardship

We share what we have and use it wisely.

03

Submission

We accept, with humility, what we still need to learn.

04

Excellence

We give our best to whatever is in front of us.

02Global Pathways Academy

A classical school
without walls.

A Christ-centered classical high school, taught in English and fully online. Students join from any country and any time zone, and are formed in mind, in character and in faith.

Enter the Academy
Grades 9–12High school
Fully onlineFrom any time zone
EnglishLanguage of instruction
RollingStart any time of year

Self-paced.
Never alone.

Coursework fits the student's own schedule. The people around the coursework are what make it a school.

01

A dedicated Advisor

Every student meets one-on-one with their own Advisor each week. Advisors guide each student's formation.

02

Asynchronous study

Lessons do not run on a bell. Students work through them on their own schedule and at their own pace.

03

Real teachers grading

Every course is graded by Academy teachers.

04

An in-house AI tutor

Built by the Academy, with content safeguards, to support students between sessions.

Curriculum

Classical in content, built on the Trivium.

Courses run on Buzz, a school-grade learning platform. A family can enroll in one course, a full diploma track, or anything in between.

  • Trivium-based coursework
  • Single course or full diploma track
  • Open to families and to partner schools that want a classical Christian course provider
  • Tuition helps fund scholarships and new classrooms for underserved students

04GP Immersion · Costa Rica

Ten days where the rainforest
meets the Pacific.

A Christ-centered immersion in Manuel Antonio, Costa Rica. It brings the Academy's classical Christian curriculum together with full cultural immersion: students live, learn and grow in community.

Enter GP Immersion
10 daysOn the ground
Manuel AntonioPacific coast, Costa Rica
1 : 10Adult chaperones to students
2027Inquiries open, by invitation

The ten days,
one at a time.

Pick a day to see what it is given to.

Day 01

Arrival and Orientation

What students do

Science in the water, service on the shore, dinner with a host family.

FaithServiceLeadershipFarm-to-TableScience and AdventureLife SkillsCulture and Spanish
  • Marine science testing and rainforest ecology
  • Cacao processing and a butchery presentation
  • A surf lesson, mangrove kayaking and canopy zip-lining
  • Fishing boat outings
  • Church services and host family dinners
  • Wildlife rescue and beach cleanup

Who comes

Designed for high school students. Open to groups of many kinds.

Schools, churches, youth groups and families can inquire, from a single family to groups of more than forty students. Each group brings one adult chaperone for every ten students. Places for 2027 are by invitation.
